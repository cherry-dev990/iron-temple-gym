World-Class Equipments

Strength

Plate-Loaded Chest Press

Commercial grade iso-lateral press for targeting upper and mid pectoral muscle fibers.

Primary Exercise: Iso-Lateral Incline Chest Press

Target Muscles: Pectoralis Major (Upper Chest), Anterior Deltoids, Triceps.

Form Tip: Retract your shoulder blades and pin them flat against the bench. Push slowly through your feet and press in a natural arc without locking out your elbows.

Power

Olympic Squat Cage

Heavy-duty power rack equipped with Olympic bars and bumper plates.

Primary Exercise: Barbell Back Squat & Overhead Press

Target Muscles: Quadriceps, Glutes, Hamstrings, Core.

Form Tip: Rest the bar on your traps, brace your core and drive through your heels.

Cardio & HIIT

Air Bikes & Rower Deck

High-resistance wind bikes and rowing machines for intense cardio.

Primary Exercise: HIIT Rower Sprints

Target Muscles: Cardiovascular System, Lats, Quads, Hamstrings, Core.

Form Tip: Drive with your legs first, lean back slightly and finish with your arms.

Functional

Dual Cable Crossover

Multi-angle adjustable pulley system with dual weight stacks.

Primary Exercise: Cable Chest Flys & Face Pulls

Target Muscles: Chest, Rear Delts, Rotator Cuff.

Form Tip: Keep a slight bend in the elbows and squeeze the chest at the end of every rep.
